Welcome to Bloody Regolith. Here a few things to know about the game.

To start the game type launch into the bar below the indroduction.

This is your character: 🧑‍🚀. You can use the arrow keys or WASD to move. In the center of your screen is your map.

There are two important tiles to know on your map. Firstly the ice ⬜, and the metal ⚙️ tile. When you are on one of these tiles you can build a mine. That will produce that resource.

To preform actions such as building you can press the buttons in the bottom right of your screen.

The small number in the bottom left of cities and players is the amount of soldiers they have.

To make soldiers you must be standing on a city and then press the train soldier button.

When a soldier is trained it is automatically stationed at the city. However you can use the < button in the bottom left. And to move a soldier from your army to the city you can use the > button.

Strategy Guide:

Firstly you should make three metal mines, then go to one of the poles to make three to five water mines.

Then you should make about four soldiers and go attack another faction.

Next using the resources you gained, fight the last faction and win.
